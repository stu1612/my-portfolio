## The problem

Case studies, posts and the Now page are MDX files in the repo. A missing field or a typo in frontmatter should stop the build with a clear message, not show up as a broken page in production.

## Typed collections

Content Collections reads `content/`, checks each file's frontmatter against a Zod schema and generates typed data. Unknown keys fail too, so a misspelt field never passes silently.

<Callout type="decision">
  I compile MDX with `@mdx-js/mdx` instead of the collections' own MDX package.
  That package pulled in dependencies with `npm audit` findings I couldn't fix.
</Callout>

## Drafts

Every entry has a `draft` flag. Drafts show in development and are left out of production builds, through one filter in one module.
